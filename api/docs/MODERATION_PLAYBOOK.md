# Rally Closed Beta Moderation Playbook

Status: internal founder-review draft. This is an operating guide, not legal advice or an emergency-response service.

## Principles

- Put immediate safety ahead of marketplace growth.
- Collect and expose the minimum information needed to decide a case.
- Do not promise confidentiality that cannot be maintained, anonymity, or a particular outcome.
- Avoid investigating beyond Rally's role. Preserve records and refer emergencies or credible imminent threats to appropriate authorities.
- Record every status change and resolution note in the founder console.

## Intake

1. Confirm the report identifies an existing user and, when Rally-linked, the reporter actually participated in that Rally.
2. Move new reports to `REVIEWING` when work begins.
3. Read only the relevant account, Rally, cancellation, feedback, block, audit, and prior-report records.
4. Do not contact the reported person with the reporter's private narrative or reveal a private Rally Again answer.
5. If the report describes immediate danger, direct the reporter to 911 or appropriate campus/public-safety resources. Rally cannot dispatch help.

Exact manual-report retries within the same UTC day are deduplicated. Each account may create up
to five distinct manual reports in a rolling 24-hour window; negative post-Rally safety checks use
their own confidential escalation path and are not blocked by this manual-report limit. A user who
reaches the limit should use the monitored Rally support channel for additional help.

## Triage

### Critical

Credible threats of violence, stalking with immediate risk, sexual violence, weapon threats, or imminent self-harm. Suspend the reported account immediately, preserve relevant records, tell the reporter to contact emergency services, and obtain qualified legal/safety guidance.

### High

Harassment, coercion, impersonation, repeated boundary violations, severe hate conduct, or attempts to evade a block. Suspend while reviewing when the allegation is credible or risk remains active.

### Standard

No-shows, spam, misleading profile details, poor sportsmanship, or lower-severity first incidents. Review history and context; use a warning, restriction, or suspension proportionate to the behavior.

## Decisions

- `RESOLVED`: action or documented no-action decision is complete.
- `DISMISSED`: information does not support a policy violation, is duplicative, or falls outside Rally's scope.
- Suspension: use when continued access presents material safety, integrity, or repeat-abuse risk. Suspending revokes active refresh sessions.
- Reactivation: require a documented reason and do not reactivate deleted accounts.

Resolution notes should state the relevant policy category, records reviewed, decision, action, and follow-up without unnecessary sensitive detail.

## Evidence And Privacy

- Keep evidence in approved, access-controlled systems; never personal notes, public issues, or chat screenshots shared outside the case.
- Do not request government IDs, UINs, exact residence, live location, medical records, or intimate images.
- Do not download or redistribute sensitive material unless necessary and lawful.
- Retain reports, audit records, and backups only for the reviewed retention period established before beta expansion.

## Follow-Up

Check for related open reports, block evasion, or active Rallies that require cancellation. Close the report in the founder console, record any account action, and monitor for recurrence. Escalate uncertain high-risk cases to qualified counsel or a safety professional rather than improvising.

## Before Inviting Testers

The founder must add a monitored support/privacy contact, choose response expectations, define retention periods, verify emergency/campus resource links, and obtain appropriate legal review of the privacy notice, guidelines, consent language, and moderation process.
