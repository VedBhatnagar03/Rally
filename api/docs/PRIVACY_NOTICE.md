# Rally Closed Beta Privacy Notice

Status: founder-review draft, policy version `2026-10-12-beta`. Add an effective date and monitored privacy contact before publishing. Obtain appropriate legal review before a broad public launch.

## The Short Version

Rally uses the information needed to help eligible UIUC students find compatible racket-sport partners, arrange a public-campus meetup, give private feedback, and use safety tools. Rally does not need a UIN, exact residence, continuous location, or access to private university systems.

## Information Rally Collects

- Account information: Illinois email address, hashed password, verification and account status, and security activity.
- Profile information: display name, age, major, class year, gender, dating preferences, intent, bio, campus area, photos, sports, skill, intensity, and recurring availability.
- Rally activity: recommendations, invitations, responses, proposed times, venue, booking status, cancellation information, and completion state.
- Feedback and safety information: the feedback a user submits, Rally Again choice, blocks, and reports. Reports and non-mutual Rally Again answers are not shown to the other participant.
- Product and security information: selected analytics events, request identifiers, audit events, last-active time, and sanitized operational incidents. Unexpected-error records exclude request bodies, authorization headers, stack traces, query strings, and exception messages.

## How Rally Uses Information

Rally uses this information to:

- create and protect accounts;
- calculate compatible, explainable recommendations;
- coordinate Rally invitations, scheduling, and venue handoff;
- reveal Rally Again only when the result is mutual;
- enforce blocks, investigate reports, and operate the closed beta;
- understand funnel performance and diagnose failures; and
- comply with valid legal obligations and protect users or the service.

Rally does not use machine-learning ranking in the closed beta and does not sell personal information.

## Who Can See Information

- Other eligible users can see the profile details needed for discovery and Rally coordination.
- A Rally participant can see shared Rally logistics and the other participant's public profile information.
- Rally's founder/admin can access account, Rally, report, audit, and operational information when needed to operate and protect the beta.
- Hosting, database, email, analytics, and monitoring providers process limited information to provide their services under Rally's configuration.
- Information may be disclosed when legally required or reasonably necessary to address fraud, abuse, or an immediate safety threat.

Rally is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official service of the University of Illinois. An `@illinois.edu` address is an eligibility signal, not a safety guarantee.

## Retention And Deletion

- Active account data is kept while the closed beta operates and while the account remains active.
- Deactivation disables access and revokes sessions without immediately deleting the account.
- Account deletion removes profile, sport, availability, photo, token, block, analytics, and private feedback-note data and anonymizes the retained account identity.
- Shared Rally records, safety reports, report outcomes, and limited audit records may be retained after deletion to protect participants, investigate abuse, and preserve operational integrity.
- The founder must set and document concrete retention periods for reports, audit logs, backups, and operational incidents before the beta expands.

## User Choices

Users can edit their profile, block or unblock another user, cancel a Rally, request an account data export, deactivate their account, or permanently delete it. A deletion request cannot be undone after processing.

## Security

Rally uses email verification, Argon2id password hashing, short-lived access tokens, rotating refresh tokens, rate limits, access controls, audit logging, and encrypted production infrastructure. No internet service can promise absolute security. Users should report suspected account compromise promptly through the published privacy contact.

## Safety And Emergencies

Rally is not an emergency service and does not monitor meetings in real time. For immediate danger, contact 911 or the appropriate campus/public-safety service. Users can also block, report, and cancel through Rally.

## Changes And Contact

Material changes should be communicated to beta participants before they take effect. Before publication, replace this paragraph with a monitored privacy/support email, effective date, and the operator's legally appropriate identity and mailing information.
