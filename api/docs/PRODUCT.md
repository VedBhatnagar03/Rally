# Rally Product

## Positioning

Rally is activity-first dating for UIUC students.

Tagline: Don't match. Rally.

Core thesis: the first interaction is not endless messaging. It is doing something together.

## Beta Scope

Must ship:

- Illinois email registration and verification.
- 18+ declaration in onboarding copy and product policy.
- Profile with name, photos, class year, major, gender, dating preferences, intent, bio, prompts, interests, sports, skill, intensity, availability.
- Curated discovery with readable reasons.
- Discovery shows actionable candidates rather than people who already have an active Rally with the viewer.
- Send Rally, accept, decline, cancel, scheduled/upcoming state.
- Completion and post-Rally feedback unlock only once the scheduled Rally has started.
- Shared time suggestions from availability overlap.
- Venue/court handoff without Active Illini automation.
- Confirmed court handoffs cannot be overwritten by the other participant.
- Cancelled Rallies retain confirmed reservation details until the booking owner completes the external cancellation.
- Feedback, safety check, Rally Again, block, report.
- Negative post-Rally safety checks are confidentially escalated to the founder moderation queue.
- Founder console for users, Rallies, reports, and funnel counts.

Explicit exclusions:

- Native mobile apps.
- Payments.
- Full chat.
- Machine learning ranking.
- Automated university booking.
- Friend mode, doubles, tournaments, feeds, or multi-campus expansion.

## Beta North Star

Completed Rallies per weekly active user.
