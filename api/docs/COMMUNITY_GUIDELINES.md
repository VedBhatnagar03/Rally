# Rally Closed Beta Community Guidelines

Status: founder-review draft, policy version `2026-10-12-beta`. Publish these guidelines before inviting participants.

## Who Rally Is For

Rally's closed beta is for adults age 18 or older who are eligible through an Illinois email address and who want to meet through racket sports. Email verification does not confirm identity, relationship status, intentions, or safety.

## Play Fair

- Use accurate, current profile information and only your own photos.
- Respect dating preferences, boundaries, skill differences, and a clear no.
- Keep first Rallies at the public campus facility shown in the plan.
- Arrive sober enough to participate safely and follow facility rules.
- Communicate cancellations promptly. Repeated no-shows may lead to removal.
- Do not pressure anyone to move locations, share private contact details, drink, use substances, spend money, or continue interacting.

## Prohibited Conduct

Rally does not allow:

- threats, stalking, harassment, bullying, hate, or discriminatory conduct;
- sexual harassment, coercion, unwanted sexual content, or non-consensual contact;
- impersonation, deceptive identity claims, scams, spam, or commercial solicitation;
- sharing another person's private information or images without permission;
- weapons, violence, dangerous play, or intentional property damage;
- circumventing a block, suspension, or account restriction; or
- using Rally while under 18.

## Safety Tools

Users may cancel any Rally, block another user, and submit a private report. Blocking immediately removes the pair from discovery and cancels any active Rally between them. Reports go to the founder/admin and are not shown to the reported person as user-authored content.

Rally Again answers stay private unless both participants choose yes. A mutual result is not consent to contact, a relationship, or any activity beyond the Rally.

## Enforcement

Rally may warn, restrict, suspend, or remove an account based on severity, available information, repeated behavior, and risk to others. Severe threats, violence, impersonation, or sexual misconduct may result in immediate suspension while reviewed. Knowingly false or retaliatory reports may also lead to action.

## Emergencies

Rally is not an emergency service and does not monitor Rallies in real time. If someone is in immediate danger, contact 911 or the appropriate campus/public-safety service first. Preserve relevant messages or details and report the conduct to Rally when safe to do so.
